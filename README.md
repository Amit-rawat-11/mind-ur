# Mind-ur

**Mind-ur** is a premium, AI-powered wellness and productivity application designed to help you organize your mind and life. Built with Flutter, it combines modern design, smart journalling, and AI assistance to create a seamless user experience.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Aurora AI**: Your intelligent companion for conversations, insights, and support.
- **Smart Journaling**: Capture your thoughts with a beautiful editor. Track your streaks and reflect on your days.
- **Habit Tracking**: Build and maintain healthy habits with customizable reminders and progress tracking.
- **Activity Feed**: Stay on top of your achievements, streaks, and summaries with a real-time activity hub.
- **Nutrition & Food Logging**: Track your meals and nutrition goals effortlessly.
- **Premium Design**: A calming, aesthetic UI featuring "Sage Mint" theming, glassmorphism, and smooth animations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Flutter (Mobile & Web)
- **Backend / Services**: Firebase (Firestore, Auth, Analytics)
- **AI Integration**: Custom AI implementation for Aurora
- **Local Storage**: Shared Preferences
- **State Management**: Provider / Riverpod patterns
